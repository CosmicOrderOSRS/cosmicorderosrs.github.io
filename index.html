🌌 About Cosmic Order

Welcome to Cosmic Order! We are a vibrant, welcoming Old School RuneScape social and PvM clan dedicated to creating a relaxed, inclusive community for players of all levels and account types. Whether you're taking your first steps in Gielinor, looking for friendly learner raid groups, or chasing end-game bossing achievements, our community is built to support your journey.

⚔️ PvM & Bossing

  • Learner Raids: Friendly runs for CoX, ToA, and ToB.
  • Bossing Mass Events: Nightmare, Nex, Corp, and God Wars.
  • PvM Mentorship: Gear advice, prayer flicking, and tile setups.
  • Bingo & Competitions: Team PvM tile races with big pot payouts.

✨ Social & Community

  • Skill of the Week (SotW): Regular XP competitions.
  • Casual Events: Hide & Seek, Quiz Nights, and Minigame Runs.
  • Active Voice Chats: Friendly bantering while grinding.
  • Chill Atmosphere: Zero toxicity policy — all playstyles welcome.

📜 Clan Requirements

  • Account Type: Main, GIM, Ironmen, and Pure friendly.
  • Discord: Joining our Discord server is required for ranks.
  • Behavior: Respect all clan members and follow Jagex Rules.
  • Activity: Stay active in CC or Discord voice/text channels.
